### Problem Brief 
Problem Statement: Many students face complex syllabi and struggle with time management and academic organization.

Target User: Students, primarily middle and high school.

Why Current Solutions Fall Short: Not many current frameworks exist targeted for students, and attempting to use a regular AI models yield general advice and fail to account for school schedules and individual learning paces.

My App Will...: After an analysis of the student's course catalog, strengths and struggles, and activities, my app will allow students to upload PDFs of upcoming assignment info (like rubrics and directions), then analyze the timeframe and work required to break down big projects into small tasks and create a day-by-day timetable to work on courses for different classes. Future integration directly into school services could be possible.

Scope for 10 Days: To create a simple web dashboard and use AI to parse text from files and understand the student's situation, and provide a calendar that can be updated as new assignments are added.
